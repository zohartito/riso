# Prompting the cobalt block-print lane

> **Internal study lane.** Use
> [`prompt-authored-illustrations.md`](prompt-authored-illustrations.md) and the
> `blueprint-coral` preset for public-candidate work. This document is retained
> to show how the prompt/preset boundary was derived; the cobalt study should
> not be presented as an original public series.

The creative contract is simple:

- **You author the subject, scene, viewpoint and organization.**
- The `cobalt-blockprint` preset contributes only medium mechanics: flat cut
  paper, two inks, adjacency-only forms, no outlines, gradients, shading or text.
- The style reference is an object-free image generated by `riso.motif`. The
  original inspiration images are never uploaded to ComfyUI or consumed by the
  preset.
- The `egypt-cobalt` palette contributes color after generation. Redux does not
  reliably transfer reference color.

## Prompt anatomy

```text
[your subject] + [viewpoint] + [how it repeats/fills the frame] + [spatial action]
```

Dense organization matters because Flux/Redux still struggles with one isolated
object in open space. Describe the image you want, not the artist or the source
motifs.

## Original starting prompts

```text
a night shift of radio astronomers carrying dish components across a desert plateau, repeated figures seen from above, filling the frame in diagonal movement

workers moving modular housing panels through a construction yard, repeated silhouettes organized in staggered rows, elevated oblique view

a flock of paper kites crossing stepped rooftops, dense edge-to-edge composition seen from above, alternating clusters and open lanes

rows of folding chairs and umbrellas after a summer concert, no people, dense all-over field seen from a high balcony

wind-bent desert plants surrounding small weather instruments, repeated clusters arranged in broad contour bands, top-down view
```

Run any one with:

```bash
riso gen "<your prompt>" --style cobalt-blockprint --ar 2:3 --seed 1
```

To inspect the exact text that will reach Flux without starting ComfyUI:

```bash
riso gen "<your prompt>" --style cobalt-blockprint --show-prompt
```

The output should equal your prompt followed by this mechanical suffix:

```text
, flat two-ink block print made from matte cut-paper shapes, solid colour fields, forms defined only by colour adjacency, no outlines, no linework, no gradients, no shading, no text
```

## Do not put these in the prompt

- the inspiration artist's name
- “in the style of”
- pyramids, sarcophagi, moon-over-water, or radiating stripes unless those are
  independently part of the subject you genuinely chose
- “editorial illustration” — the existing measurements show that noun makes
  Flux draw contour lines

## Compute boundary

`riso gen` is the prompt-driven lane and still needs the PC generation box:

```text
your prompt + generic medium suffix
        ↓
Flux schnell + internal object-free Redux reference on the PC
        ↓
crop to the densest authored region
        ↓
local cobalt palette separation + grain + misregistration
```

`riso motif` remains the no-PC lane for explicitly procedural compositions.
It complements prompting; it does not replace it.
