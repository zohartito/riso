# Field Exceptions prompt series

This is an original architectural response to the generic idea of repetition
plus deviation. The external “Just another day in the crowd” post contributed
only abstract observations: edge-to-edge repetition, compressed depth,
overlapping silhouettes, and one exception that changes the reading of the
whole field.

Excluded from the new work: commuters, passengers, auditorium seats, phones,
business clothing, green skin or upholstery, copied gestures or expressions,
the source palette, every source composition, visible seals, signatures, and
the creator's name in generation prompts.

## Original prompts

```text
a dense field of repeated sawtooth roof monitors viewed from high oblique angle, one monitor inverted to form a sheltered rain court, roof ridges overlap from edge to edge, no people

a compressed frontal view of a modular housing facade made from many identical recessed balcony bays, one double-height communal balcony bridges two bays and breaks the rhythm, facade fills the portrait frame, no people

an axonometric structural hall containing a dense forest of identical rectangular columns and shallow beams, one broad diagonal accessibility ramp threads through several bays and opens a clear void, no people

a top-down field of repeated square cistern covers and narrow drainage joints, one circular rain garden interrupts the grid and gathers several converging channels, the constructed ground fills the portrait frame, no people
```

Run with `--style field-exceptions --ar 2:3` and use one fixed seed per tile.
Every final receives a generation receipt and a deterministic `riso stylize`
sidecar.

## Source boundary

The linked X images are inspection-only. They are not supplied to image
generation, uploaded to ComfyUI, stored as a shipped reference, or named in a
prompt. The style reference is an object-free image generated by `riso.motif`,
and the palette is a new project selection. This supports an independent-process
claim but does not guarantee exclusive copyright.
