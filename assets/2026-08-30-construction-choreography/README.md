## Alt text

1. Tall portrait print of an unfinished structural frame seen obliquely. Large
   navy bays, cream columns and coral edges surround two tiny workers at the
   bottom; fine grain and slight color misregistration mimic screen printing.
2. Axonometric modular courtyard building floating over a dark ground. Navy
   roof and walls are separated by aqua panels, cream voids and thin coral
   registration edges on a textured aqua field.
3. Top-down construction staging field arranged in three vertical lanes. Coral,
   navy and aqua component-like rectangles, circular fittings and small tools
   sit on warm paper with broad empty aisles.
4. Crane cables suspend a cream prefabricated stair-core module above an
   unfinished multi-story frame. Navy sky and frame, coral panels, aqua braces
   and grain create a limited-palette architectural print.

## Truth boundary

These are fresh Flux-schnell + Redux generations made from Zohar-authored prompts,
then reduced by `riso` to a project-selected palette with seeded grain and
misregistration. The inspiration post informed earlier research but was not
uploaded, referenced, or named in this preset. That supports an independent
process claim; it does not guarantee exclusive copyright. The Redux adapter is
licensed for non-commercial use, so these images must not be represented as
commercially unrestricted.

The raw pre-post-pass images are retained beside the finals. Each JSON sidecar
contains the seed, size, model, adapter, internal reference and exact prompt.
